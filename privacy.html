How HealthPlus protects your personal data, secures your payments, and respects your rights.

HealthPlus Pharmacy ("HealthPlus", "we", "us", "our") operates the HealthPlus website and mobile-accessible platform at healthplusmedical.com. We are an online pharmacy selling medicines and health products, with prescription verification and delivery services primarily in Accra, Ghana, and to international customers worldwide.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and what rights you have over your information. By creating a HealthPlus account or placing an order, you agree to the practices described here.

📋1. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

1.1 Account & Identity Information

  • Full name, date of birth, gender
  • Email address and phone number (including international dial code)
  • Country of residence
  • Ghana Card number (optional — for Ghanaian customer identity verification)
  • Customer ID (generated by HealthPlus on registration)
  • Password (stored as a one-way cryptographic hash — we never store your plain-text password)

1.2 Prescription & Order Information

  • Prescription images or PDFs you upload for prescription-only medicines
  • Verification notes from our licensed pharmacists (shared only with you)
  • Order history, items purchased, and delivery details
Special category data

Prescription information can reveal details about your health and is treated as "special category" or "sensitive" personal data under data protection law. We process it only to verify and fulfil your order, and only with your consent.

1.3 Transaction & Payment Information

  • Order details, amounts, and payment method chosen
  • Mobile Money network and account name (not your PIN — we never receive or store your PIN)
  • Card payment is processed via PCI-DSS compliant third-party processors (Stripe, PayPal, Square). HealthPlus never stores your card number, CVV, or expiry date on our servers.
  • Transaction reference numbers for reconciliation

1.4 Technical & Usage Information

  • IP address and approximate geolocation (country/city level)
  • Browser type, operating system, and device type
  • Pages visited, features used, and session duration (via our own analytics — no third-party ad trackers)
  • Error logs for debugging (anonymised within 30 days)

🎯2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Fulfilling orders — processing your cart, verifying prescriptions, and dispatching deliveries
  • Customer identification — matching you to your account, orders and prescription records
  • Payment processing — charging for orders and issuing refunds
  • Order communication — sending email confirmations, delivery updates, and order status notifications
  • Security — detecting fraud, preventing unauthorised access, and investigating suspicious activity
  • Legal compliance — meeting our obligations under Ghanaian data protection law and applicable international regulations
  • Service improvement — aggregated, anonymised usage analytics to improve the platform

We do not sell your personal information to any third party. We do not use your prescription or health-related data for advertising purposes.

🔒3. SSL Encryption & Technical Security

All data transmitted between your browser or device and the HealthPlus platform is encrypted using TLS 1.3 (Transport Layer Security), the same standard used by banks. You will see a padlock icon in your browser's address bar confirming the connection is secure.

  • 256-bit AES encryption for data at rest in our databases
  • HTTPS enforced on every page — HTTP connections are automatically redirected
  • HSTS (HTTP Strict Transport Security) headers prevent protocol downgrade attacks
  • Content Security Policy (CSP) headers prevent cross-site scripting (XSS) injection
  • X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN prevents clickjacking attacks
  • Regular security audits — our codebase is reviewed quarterly by an independent security team
  • Penetration testing — annual external penetration tests on all production systems
  • Access controls — staff access to customer and prescription data is role-based, logged, and audited

💳4. Payment Security

HealthPlus accepts multiple payment methods, each with specific security measures:

Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo)

Mobile Money payments use a network-initiated USSD prompt sent directly to your phone. HealthPlus receives only a payment confirmation reference — we never receive or store your MoMo PIN. Your PIN is between you and your mobile network only.

Card Payments (Stripe)

Card payments are processed directly by Stripe, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor — the highest level of payment security certification. Your card number, CVV, and expiry date are transmitted directly to Stripe's servers and are never seen by or stored on HealthPlus infrastructure. HealthPlus receives only a tokenised reference and transaction status.

PayPal

PayPal payments are processed entirely within PayPal's secure environment. HealthPlus does not handle any PayPal account credentials or payment details.

Transaction Fees

The following processing fees may be applied to transactions:

  • Mobile Money: 1.5% network processing fee (applied by the mobile network)
  • Stripe (card): 2.9% + USD 0.30 per transaction (industry standard)
  • PayPal: 3.49% + fixed fee depending on currency
  • Square: 2.6% + USD 0.10 per transaction
  • Transaction fees are displayed clearly at checkout before payment is confirmed. HealthPlus does not add markup to processor fees.

⚖️5. Your Data Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data held by HealthPlus:

  • Right of access — request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to legal retention obligations for medical records)
  • Right to restrict processing — ask us to limit how we use your data while a dispute is resolved
  • Right to data portability — receive your health data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent (e.g. marketing emails), withdraw at any time without affecting prior processing
  • Right to object — object to processing for legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@healthplusmedical.com. We will respond within 30 days.

🍪6. Cookies & Local Storage

HealthPlus uses the following types of storage in your browser:

  • Session storage — stores your cart, session ID, and security event log for the duration of your browser session. Cleared automatically when you close the tab.
  • Strictly necessary cookies — required for the site to function (e.g. session authentication). Cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — our own first-party analytics (no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel). Collects anonymised page-view data to improve the service. You may opt out by contacting us.

We do not use advertising cookies, remarketing pixels, or cross-site tracking cookies of any kind.

🤝7. Third-Party Services

HealthPlus uses the following trusted third-party services to operate the platform:

  • Stripe — card payment processing (PCI-DSS Level 1)
  • PayPal — alternative payment processing
  • Square — alternative payment processing
  • OpenStreetMap / Leaflet — map tiles for live order delivery tracking. No personal data is sent to OpenStreetMap.
  • Google Fonts — loads Fraunces and Inter typefaces. Google may log your IP as part of font delivery. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

All third-party processors are bound by Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) requiring them to process your data only on our behalf and in accordance with this policy.

🗂️8. Data Retention

  • Account data — retained for the duration of your account, plus 2 years after closure
  • Prescription & order records — retained for 6 years as required by Ghana FDA and Pharmacy Council recordkeeping rules
  • Payment records — retained for 7 years for tax and audit purposes
  • Security logs — retained for 90 days then anonymised
  • Analytics data — aggregated after 12 months, never tied to a named individual

🌍9. International Customers

HealthPlus serves customers from all countries. If you are located outside Ghana, your personal data may be transferred to and processed in Ghana, where our servers are located. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers, including standard contractual clauses where required by the laws of your country.

For customers in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or other jurisdictions with strong data protection frameworks, we process your data in a manner compatible with GDPR principles, including lawfulness, fairness, transparency, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, and integrity and confidentiality.

👶10. Children's Privacy

HealthPlus services are not directed to children under 13, and accounts may only be created by individuals aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If we discover we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13 without consent, we will delete it promptly.
