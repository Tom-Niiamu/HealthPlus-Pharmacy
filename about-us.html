FAQ

Common Questions

How do I order medicine online?

Search or browse our catalogue on the Shop page, add items to your cart, then check out. If an item requires a prescription, you'll be prompted to upload one before payment.

Do I need a prescription for every medicine?

No. Over-the-counter items like pain relief, vitamins and first-aid products don't need one. Prescription-only medicines require a valid prescription, which our pharmacists verify before dispensing.

What areas does HealthPlus deliver to?

We deliver to all major areas of Accra including East Legon, Adenta, Airport Residential, Cantonments, Labone, Osu, Spintex, Tema, Madina, Haatso and more. If you're unsure, message us on WhatsApp.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money), Visa/Mastercard/Verve cards, and bank transfer for larger orders.

How does delivery tracking work?

Once your order is confirmed and any prescription is verified, a rider collects it from our pharmacy and delivers to your address. You can follow the whole journey live on the map from the Track Order page.

Is my personal and payment information private?

Absolutely. All orders and payments are encrypted. Your personal data and prescription images are stored securely and never shared with any third party without your consent.

What if I'm not satisfied with my order?

Your experience matters to us. If there's an issue with your order or delivery, contact us within 24 hours and we will make it right — including a replacement or refund where appropriate.
